# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents, caregivers, and professionals conducting child-development screenings, often on mobile devices and in regional languages.

## Product Purpose

Saarathi helps caregivers record child information, complete developmental screening questionnaires, and understand the resulting guidance in a supportive, accessible experience.

## Brand Personality

Warm, reassuring, family-friendly. The interface should feel clear and conversational without losing trust or healthcare seriousness.

## Anti-references

Avoid clinical jargon, intimidating diagnostic language, dense untranslated screens, and layouts that break when regional-language text is longer than English.

## Design Principles

- Use plain, conversational language that families can understand.
- Preserve the calm, supportive flow across languages.
- Keep consent, privacy, and screening limitations explicit.
- Adapt spacing and wrapping for regional scripts without changing the product structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, and Tamil consistently across all screens and reusable components. Preserve readable type, adequate contrast, touch targets, and layouts that accommodate longer translated strings.
